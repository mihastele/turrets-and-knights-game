How it works

  • Both commanders spend coins on troops, turrets and factories.
  • Troops march lane by lane. Break through the enemy breach line to win.
  • You start with 100 coins, get +10 every 5s, plus a shared windfall of 5–10 every 12–20s.
  • A factory (+5/5s) pays off in 55s — greedily building too early opens you to an early rush.
  • At 6:00 overtime starts: no new turrets, turrets lose 15% damage every 30s. At 10:00 the match is a draw.
  • Got a coupon? Redeem it below the room buttons to unlock the Dragon and lane-wide field effects.
